# LAKRIDS BY BÜLOW

> Danish gourmet liquorice maker founded by Johan Bülow in Svaneke, Bornholm on 07.07.2007. Trades as Lakrids by Johan Bülow A/S (CVR 33041586), Sydholmen 5, 2650 Hvidovre, Denmark. Sells plain and chocolate-coated liquorice direct to consumers across Europe and through retail partners, concept stores and B2B/corporate gifting.

## Status

LAKRIDS BY BÜLOW does not operate a public developer program. There is no
first-party API documentation, no developer portal, no SDKs in any public
package registry, and no published OpenAPI, AsyncAPI or MCP surface. Its
website runs on the BigCommerce Stencil platform, whose storefront APIs are
reachable on the brand's own host but are documented by BigCommerce, not by
LAKRIDS BY BÜLOW.

## APIs

- [BigCommerce Storefront GraphQL API](https://developer.bigcommerce.com/docs/storefront/graphql): platform-provided GraphQL endpoint at https://lakridsbybulow.com/graphql. Requires a storefront bearer token; probed unauthenticated and rejected with 401.
- [BigCommerce Storefront REST API](https://developer.bigcommerce.com/docs/rest-storefront): platform-provided REST surface at https://lakridsbybulow.com/api/storefront. Session-scoped to the shopper's browser session.
